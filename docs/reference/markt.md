# Markt: reference notes from the owner's photos (2026-10-24)

The photos stay out of the repo (they show passers-by); these notes are what we take from them.

**Facades (most important)**
- Dominant materials: **blue-grey limestone** (hardsteen) plinths, pilasters and window surrounds,
  **grey/white/cream plaster**, some dark red brick with stone dressings. Much less red brick and
  ochre than our current heuristics produce.
- Windows: tall, **white frames, many panes** (2×3 or 2×4 per sash), transoms with small panes,
  stone surrounds; ground floors with large shop windows and lettering in the upper panes.
- **Wrought-iron balconies** on first floors (ornate), simple iron railings on french windows.
- Doors: **white panelled doors with a fanlight**, stone door surround, house number.
- Plinth: limestone with **cellar vent grilles**.
- Cornices: stone, projecting; pediments over some windows (classical).
- Roofs: **dark grey slate** (mansard and gable), **white dormers** with small pitched roofs,
  chimneys.

**Street**
- **Dark cobbles** (grey-brown setts), dark stone kerbs, granite bench blocks.
- **Plane and horse-chestnut trees**: tall bare trunks, large irregular crowns, fairy lights
  wrapped round trunks in season.
- **Long rows of parked bikes** along the tree line (dozens, all colours).
- Black cast-iron **lamp posts**, double-headed lanterns on the Markt; cast-iron bollards with chains.
- Delivery vans (yellow DHL, white vans) and trailers parked on the square, market stall frames
  (grey modular stalls) stored at the edge.

**Stadhuis (hero landmark)**
- Grey limestone, symmetric classical facade, ground-floor arcade of 3 arches with a double
  staircase, balustrade balcony, a pediment with carved relief, hipped dark roof.
- Octagonal **bell tower** on the roof: lead-grey, open arcaded stages, a gold clock face, a small
  dome and lantern with a weather vane.

**Stadhuis, close photo (2026-10-24)**: not a bland block. Grey limestone with darker stone
courses and quoins, giant pilasters between the bays, strong cornices at the first floor and
the eaves; a central risalit rising an attic storey above the wings with a triangular pediment
holding a carved (yellowish) relief and carved ornaments on its upper corners; pediments on
the side fronts too. Ground floor centre: three tall round arches on piers (a loggia) reached
by a double external stair with stone balustrades; above it a full-width balustraded balcony.
Tall white windows (2 x 3 small panes) in stone surrounds, small cellar windows. Wrought-iron
railings with ornate gates at the front, lamp posts. Large hipped dark slate roof with lead
ridges, a dormer on the tower's base. Tower in lead/blue-grey: square base, an octagonal stage
with tall arched windows above a row of oval oculi, the clock stage with gold dials and small
round windows, a projecting balustraded gallery with flags, the open belfry (bells visible), a
bell-shaped dome, a small lantern, a gilded orb and a weathervane.
