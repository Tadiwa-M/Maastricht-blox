# Concept sheets (made outside this repo)

The owner commissions these from a separate image AI. They are the **target** for renders: a look is
judged against its sheet, not against a text description (the video's lesson: "give the AI a picture to aim for").
Mockups still come first for any look change (CLAUDE.md).

## What to commission, per look (Classic, Spider-Verse, Manga, and each next world)
1. **Key art of the city:** the Markt at day, and at night. Shows light, sky, shadow colour and ink or print treatment.
2. **Figure turnaround:** front, side, back, in the stance and one dramatic pose. Face, hair, build, outfit.
3. **Vehicles:** the bus, a car, a bike, the river boat.
4. **UI language:** a panel or caption sample, the HUD, the menu card.
5. **Palette strip:** 6 to 8 swatches (sky top and horizon, shade, light, accent).

## Rules
- Original designs only: no characters, logos or art of existing series (the same rule as the figures).
- Pictures stay out of git unless the owner says so (size and licence). `manifest.md` lists each approved sheet:
  file name, look, the tool and prompt that made it, the date, and "approved by the owner".
- Both engines are measured against the same approved sheets.
